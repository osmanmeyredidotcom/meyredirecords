      Across instruments.
      Between genres.

      Osman Meyredi is an Italian-born multi-instrumentalist, songwriter, composer, singer, music director and producer based in Amsterdam. He moves easily between instruments and genres, bringing different styles and influences together into songs that feel personal, honest and never quite predictable.

      Trained as a classical double bassist, he has toured with Frank Zappa’s longtime vocalist Ike Willis and shared the stage with Blues Brothers saxophonist Blue Lou Marini.

      With Meyredi RecordsIn October 2026 he co-launched his own label, Meyredi Records, starting with a reworked version of his single “Dance with this Mess”, a song that turns the chaos of life into a rhythm you can move to.

      01 / Release & promote

      Label/demo
      submissions

      Discuss a release or demo ↗

      Finished music, ready for its next chapter.

      We consider selected recordings for release and promotion through Meyredi Records. An artist can bring a finished single while continuing to work with their own manager on the wider career.

      • Release planning and distribution coordination
      • Artwork, credits and promotional content
      • Press outreach and radio pitches
      • Release-related income within the agreed arrangement

      Submitting music is a request for consideration. Release support and artist management are separate arrangements.

      02 / Develop & record

      Music
      Production

      Discuss your recording ↗

      From an idea or song to a finished recording.

      Osman Meyredi brings composition, multiple instruments and production together. Musical ideas can develop during the recording process, with outside collaborators involved where the project needs them.

      • Musical direction and arranging
      • Instruments and vocals
      • Recording and editing
      • Mixing and mastering

      You can work with us on production and release independently or through another label. Share a music link and tell us where your project stands.

      03 / Career & business

      Artist
      management

      Discuss artist management ↗

      A longer view of your career.

      For a selective group of artists, management connects creative direction with business opportunities. Jolene Prins brings business leadership and strategy to the relationships, decisions and plans around the music.

      • Career planning, audiences and live opportunities
      • Finding partners, including booking representation
      • Evaluating offers, collaborations and negotiating terms
      • Coordinating teams and connecting releases with longer-term plans

      The scope is set within a management agreement. Recordings can still be released and promoted by another label.
